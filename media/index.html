Bio and photo

About Suman

Suman Smith is the author of Stop Cock-Blocking Your Dating Life and the creator of Lift the Velvet Rope, a three-step method for telling real standards apart from old fears.

She grew up in a culture where love was decided for you. At 24, she said no to Oprah, then went on her show to talk about choosing love for yourself. Years of long-distance relationships and a dating-app ban at 50 followed, until she reconnected with her Grade 3 crush and married him at 54.

Since 2007, she has designed engagement rings and listened to couples' love stories across the table. It taught her how people really choose, and what quietly gets in the way.
