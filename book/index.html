Inside, you’ll learn how to

  • Recognize the “standards” that are really self-protection
  • Stop turning silence, rejection and bad dates into proof that something is wrong with you
  • Tell chemistry from familiarity, and see why calm love can feel “boring” at first
  • Stay true to yourself when you’re starting to care about someone

One practice at its heart: Lift the Velvet Rope

1. Check the list.Separate what actually happened from what you’re making it mean.
2. Feel what’s real.Let yourself feel it, without letting the story decide for you.
3. Lift the rope, or don’t.Choose your next step on purpose, not from old fear.
